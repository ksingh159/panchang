# Varanasi Panchang

**Live site: [varanasipanchang.com](https://varanasipanchang.com)** · [हिंदी](https://varanasipanchang.com/hi/)

A free Hindu panchang and jyotish website that follows Kashi (Varanasi) rules for festival and vrat dates. Calculations use the Swiss Ephemeris.

## What it does

- **[Daily panchang](https://varanasipanchang.com/panchang/)**: tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise and sunset, Rahu Kaal and choghadiya for any city
- **[Festival dates](https://varanasipanchang.com/festivals/)**: Ekadashi with parana times, Diwali Lakshmi puja muhurat and other vrats, each decided by Kashi rules (Smarta and Vaishnava)
- **[Grahan (eclipses)](https://varanasipanchang.com/grahan/)**: sparsha and moksha times, sutak, visibility for your city and the eclipse path on a 3D globe
- **[Shubh muhurat](https://varanasipanchang.com/shubh-muhurat/)**: auspicious dates for marriage, griha pravesh, vehicle purchase and more
- **[Kundali](https://varanasipanchang.com/kundali/create)**, **[kundali milan](https://varanasipanchang.com/kundali-milan/)**, **[gochar/transits](https://varanasipanchang.com/transits/)**, **[naamkaran](https://varanasipanchang.com/naamkaran/)** and **[aahaar](https://varanasipanchang.com/aahaar/)**

Available in English and Hindi.

## Tech

- Django (Python)
- [Swiss Ephemeris](https://www.astro.com/swisseph/) for planetary positions
- [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/) for city data and [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) for map data
