# Devuthani Ekadashi to Kharmas 2026: What Should You Complete Before Kharmas Begins?

The period between Devuthani Ekadashi and Kharmas is considered an important time in the Hindu calendar. After the completion of Chaturmas, many auspicious ceremonies and traditional activities resume with Devuthani Ekadashi. However, this period does not continue indefinitely. With the arrival of Dhanu Sankranti in December, Kharmas begins, during which several auspicious ceremonies are traditionally avoided.

In 2026, [Devuthani Ekadashi](/festivals/2026/devutthana-ekadashi/) will be observed on **20 November**, while Kharmas will begin on **16 December 2026**, with [Dhanu Sankranti](/festivals/2026/dhanu-sankranti/).

This gives families an important period to plan pending religious ceremonies, household responsibilities, family functions and other auspicious activities before Kharmas begins.

Let's understand what this period means and what you should consider completing before Kharmas.

**Duration:** Approximately 26 days between Devuthani Ekadashi and the beginning of Kharmas.

**Note on the Ekadashi date:** Because the Ekadashi tithi begins after sunrise on 20 November and ends shortly after sunrise on 21 November, households following the Smarta tradition observe the vrat on 20 November, while those following the Vaishnav tradition observe it on 21 November. Follow the practice of your family or local temple.

## 1. Why Is the Period Between Devuthani Ekadashi and Kharmas Important?

According to Hindu religious traditions, Lord Vishnu enters Yoga Nidra (divine sleep) on Devshayani Ekadashi, marking the beginning of Chaturmas. During these four months, many auspicious ceremonies, particularly weddings and certain household rituals, are traditionally put on hold.

Devuthani Ekadashi, also known as Prabodhini Ekadashi, marks the symbolic awakening of Lord Vishnu. It is considered the beginning of a period when auspicious ceremonies can resume.

[Tulsi Vivah](/festivals/2026/tulsi-vivah/), the ceremonial marriage of Tulsi Mata with Lord Vishnu in his Shaligram form, is also associated with this occasion.

However, another important transition approaches in December. When the Sun enters Sagittarius (Dhanu Rashi), Dhanu Sankranti occurs and Kharmas begins.

Therefore, the period between these occasions is traditionally considered an opportunity to complete pending auspicious ceremonies before the next religious pause.

## 4. Kharmas Mein Shubh Karya: What Should You Do During Kharmas?

Kharmas is not considered a period when religious activities come to a complete stop. In fact, many traditions place special importance on devotion, charity and spiritual discipline during this time.

Some activities traditionally encouraged during Kharmas include:

**1. Worship of Lord Vishnu**

Devotees may worship Lord Vishnu and recite Vishnu Sahasranama, perform daily puja and observe religious practices according to their family traditions.

**2. Surya Dev Worship**

Since Kharmas begins with Dhanu Sankranti, worship of Surya Dev is also significant. Offering Arghya to the Sun during the morning is a commonly followed practice.

**3. Charity and Donations**

Donating food, clothes, blankets and other essentials to people in need is considered meritorious.

**4. Religious Reading and Recitation**

Devotees may read the Bhagavad Gita, Ramcharitmanas, Vishnu Purana and other religious scriptures.

**5. Pilgrimage and Seva**

Visiting temples, participating in religious activities and helping those in need are also encouraged.

Thus, Kharmas can be observed as a period of spiritual reflection, devotion and charitable activities.

## 5. Kharmas Mein Kya Nahi Karna Chahiye: Activities Traditionally Avoided

In many North Indian Hindu traditions, certain auspicious ceremonies are generally postponed during Kharmas.

These include:

- Marriage ceremonies.
- Griha Pravesh.
- Mundan Sanskar.
- Upanayan Sanskar.
- Major auspicious inaugurations.
- Certain property-related Muhurat ceremonies.

However, customs differ across regions and communities. Some activities may have different rules depending on the tradition followed by the family.

It is therefore advisable to consult a knowledgeable priest or check the relevant Panchang before making an important religious decision.

## 6. When Will Auspicious Muhurats Resume After Kharmas?

Kharmas concludes with the Sun's transition into Capricorn (Makar Rashi), marking Makar Sankranti.

In 2027, the Sun enters Makar Rashi in the late evening of 14 January. Because the transition takes place after sunset, [Makar Sankranti](/festivals/2027/makar-sankranti/) and its Punya Kaal are observed on **Friday, 15 January 2027** in most Panchangs, including in Varanasi.

After Kharmas, auspicious ceremonies may resume, but this does not mean every date automatically becomes suitable for weddings, Griha Pravesh or other Sanskars.

The availability of Muhurats also depends on planetary positions, the visibility of Jupiter and Venus, and other relevant Panchang considerations.

Always check the specific Muhurat before finalising an important ceremony. The [Vivah Muhurat 2027](/shubh-muhurat/vivah/2027/) dates after Kharmas are already listed.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. When is Devuthani Ekadashi in 2026?

Devuthani Ekadashi will be observed on Friday, 20 November 2026, according to the Smarta tradition, and on Saturday, 21 November 2026, according to the Vaishnav tradition. It marks the traditional awakening of Lord Vishnu and the end of Chaturmas.

### 2. When does Kharmas begin in 2026?

Kharmas begins on Wednesday, 16 December 2026, with Dhanu Sankranti, when the Sun enters Sagittarius (Dhanu Rashi). It ends when the Sun enters Makar Rashi on 14 January 2027, with Makar Sankranti observed on 15 January 2027.

### 3. How many days are there between Devuthani Ekadashi and Kharmas?

There are approximately 26 calendar days between Devuthani Ekadashi on 20 November and the beginning of Kharmas on 16 December 2026.

### 4. Can we get married between Devuthani Ekadashi and Kharmas?

Yes, marriages are traditionally performed during this period, provided a suitable Vivah Muhurat is available. Wedding dates in this period are 21, 25, 26 and 30 November and 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 11 and 12 December 2026. The date and Lagna should be confirmed according to the local Panchang.

### 5. Kharmas mein kya nahi karna chahiye?

During Kharmas, marriage, Griha Pravesh, Mundan, Upanayan and major auspicious inaugurations are traditionally postponed in many North Indian traditions. Customs vary by region and family, so consult a priest or the Panchang for specific decisions.

### 6. Can we purchase a house or vehicle during Kharmas?

Ordinary purchases are not universally prohibited during Kharmas. However, certain families avoid performing major auspicious ceremonies or beginning specific ventures during this period. Traditions and Muhurat requirements should be considered.

### 7. Kharmas mein shubh karya kaun se kar sakte hain?

Kharmas is traditionally associated with worship, Surya Arghya, Vishnu Puja, charity, religious reading, pilgrimage and spiritual practices. These activities are considered meritorious during this period.
