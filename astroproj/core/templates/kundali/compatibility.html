What is Kundali Milan?

Before a marriage, the birth charts of the bride and groom are compared through eight kootas: Varna, Vashya, Tara, Yoni, Graha Maitri, Gana, Bhakoot and Nadi. Together they carry 36 gunas. A score of 18 or more is traditionally considered workable, but the score is only a beginning: doshas, their cancellations, and the strength of each chart matter too.
