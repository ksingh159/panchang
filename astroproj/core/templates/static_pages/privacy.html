Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Information we collect

  • Account data: email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and email from Google.
  • Birth details you enter: date, time and place of birth for kundali, Kundali Milan and personalised muhurat. Saved only if you are signed in and choose to save them.
  • Location and preferences: the city you select, your native state, food region and month system, stored in your profile or a session cookie.
  • Orders: when you buy a PDF, your name, email, phone (if given), the order details and the payment reference from the gateway. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Razorpay or PhonePe and never reach us.
  • Messages: what you send through the contact form, with your name and email.
  • Usage data: standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages visited) kept for security and debugging.

How we use it

Only to compute and show your panchang, charts and muhurats, keep you signed in, deliver and support your orders, answer your messages, keep the service secure, and meet legal duties such as tax records. We do not sell personal data.

Who we share it with

Only with the services that run the site: our hosting provider, the payment gateways (Razorpay, PhonePe) for orders, Google for sign-in, advertising and analytics as described below, and authorities when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Account data and saved birth details until you delete them or close your account. A generated PDF is kept for about 30 days after purchase so you can download it again, then deleted. Order records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. Contact messages are kept for up to two years. Server logs are kept for a short period for security.

Cookies and advertising

We use cookies to keep you signed in and remember your city. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies at www.aboutads.info. See how Google uses data from partner sites.

If Google Analytics is enabled, it collects usage statistics under Google's privacy policy.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask to see, correct or erase your personal data, and withdraw consent. You can edit or delete your birth details and kundalis at any time from your profile, or ask us to delete your account{% if CONTACT_EMAIL %} by writing to {{ CONTACT_EMAIL }}{% endif %}. Complaints can go to our grievance officer{% if GRIEVANCE_OFFICER %}, {{ GRIEVANCE_OFFICER }}{% endif %}, through the contact page.

Security

The site runs over HTTPS, passwords are stored hashed, and paid PDFs are encrypted and watermarked for the buyer. No system is perfectly secure, so tell us at once if you notice anything wrong.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
