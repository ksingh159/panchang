About {{ SITE_NAME }}

{{ SITE_TAGLINE }}. {{ SITE_NAME }} is a free Hindu panchang for any city: the day's tithi, nakshatra, yoga and karana; festivals and vrats decided by Kashi (Varanasi) panchang rules with the puja muhurat for your city; shubh muhurat for buying and new beginnings; every Surya and Chandra grahan with its sutak; seasonal aahaar from the Ayurvedic texts; baby names by nakshatra; and, for those who want them, janam kundali, Kundali Milan and gochar.

How our calculations work

  • Ephemeris: the Sun, Moon and planets come from the Swiss Ephemeris, the data used by professional astronomy software, with Chitrapaksha ayanamsa.
  • Panchang: tithi, nakshatra, yoga and karana are computed as exact instants and stored once per year, so any date from 1900 to 2200 can be searched instantly.
  • Festivals: Purnimanta months as used in Varanasi; each festival is fixed by the kaal in which its tithi must prevail (udaya, pradosh, nishita, madhyahna, aparahna, moonrise), following Nirnaya Sindhu and Dharma Sindhu. Each festival page names the rule that decided its date.
  • Places: 170,000+ towns from GeoNames with their time zones, so dates before Indian Standard Time or with war-time or daylight-saving offsets are handled correctly.

Our approach

We show the rule behind every date, so you can verify it with your family pandit. Where traditions differ (Smarta vs Vaishnava Ekadashi, Udaya vs Nishita Janmashtami) we show both. Every page is free to read, in English and Hindi.
