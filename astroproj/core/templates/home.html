What is Panchang?

Panchang is a Hindu calendar and almanac, which follows traditional units of Indian timekeeping. The word "Panchang" is derived from the Sanskrit words "Pancha" (five) and "Anga" (limbs), referring to the five limbs or attributes of the day.

The Five Elements of Panchang:

  • Tithi: Lunar day (30 tithis in a lunar month)
  • Nakshatra: Constellation (27 nakshatras in the zodiac)
  • Yoga: Auspicious period formed by Sun and Moon positions (27 yogas)
  • Karana: Half of a tithi (11 karanas repeated in a lunar month)
  • Vaara: Day of the week (7 days governed by different planets)

Importance in Daily Life:

Panchang is used to determine auspicious times (Muhurta) for important events such as weddings, house warming ceremonies, business ventures, and religious rituals. It helps in understanding the cosmic energies influencing each day and making decisions aligned with natural rhythms.
